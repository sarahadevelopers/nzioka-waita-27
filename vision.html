Our Vision for Machakos

A comprehensive 5-pillar plan to transform Machakos into a model African county by 2032 — built on innovation, inclusivity, and sustainable development.

The 5 Pillars of Transformation

Our comprehensive plan addresses Machakos' most pressing challenges while unlocking its full potential for future generations.

01
Pillar 1

Smart Governance

Implement digital systems for transparency, efficient service delivery, and citizen engagement through technology-driven solutions. We will leverage technology to make county government more accessible, accountable, and responsive to the needs of all Machakos residents.

Digital County Services Portal — Access all government services online
E-Procurement & Transparency Systems — Eliminate corruption and increase efficiency
Citizen Feedback & Participation Platforms — Give residents a voice in decision-making
Real-time Service Delivery Tracking — Monitor county projects and services in real time
Open Data Initiative — Publish county data for public scrutiny and innovation
02
Pillar 2

Economic Empowerment

Support youth entrepreneurship, SMEs, and create inclusive business policies to stimulate job creation and economic growth. We will build a vibrant economy that provides opportunities for all Machakos residents, from small business owners to large investors.

50,000 Youth Jobs Creation Program — Employment opportunities through skills training
SME Development Fund & Support — Access to capital for local businesses
Machakos Innovation & Tech Hub — A center for technology and entrepreneurship
Business Registration Simplified — Reduce bureaucracy and red tape
Women in Business Program — Support female entrepreneurs with mentorship and funding
03
Pillar 3

Infrastructure & Mobility

Upgrade roads, public transport, and housing solutions to address Machakos' growing urban mobility challenges. We will build the infrastructure needed to support a growing population and a thriving economy.

Modernized Public Transport System — Safe, reliable, and affordable transportation
Road Network Upgrade & Maintenance — Well-maintained roads connecting all areas
Affordable Housing Program — Quality housing for all Machakos residents
Pedestrian & Cycling Infrastructure — Safe walkways and bike lanes
Smart City Initiatives — Technology-driven urban management
04
Pillar 4

Safety & Security

Neighborhood policing, modern surveillance systems, and community safety programs to make Machakos safer for all residents. Every citizen deserves to feel safe in their home, on the streets, and in their community.

Neighborhood Policing Program — Community-based law enforcement
Modern Surveillance Systems — Technology to deter and solve crime
Community Safety Programs — Engaging residents in crime prevention
Street Lighting Initiative — Well-lit streets for safer neighborhoods
Youth Engagement Programs — Keeping young people safe and productive
05
Pillar 5

Environment & Sustainability

Green spaces expansion, waste management reforms, and climate-conscious urban planning for a cleaner, greener Machakos. We will protect our environment for future generations while creating economic opportunities in the green economy.

Green Spaces Expansion — More parks, gardens, and recreational areas
Waste Management Reforms — Sustainable waste collection and recycling
Climate-Conscious Urban Planning — Building a sustainable city
Tree Planting Initiative — Increasing Machakos' tree cover by 30%
Water Conservation Programs — Protecting our water resources
