Meet Your Candidate

Nzioka Waita

An experienced leader, technocrat, and public servant with a proven track record of excellence in governance. Nzioka Waita is committed to building a Machakos that works for everyone — from the business owner to the boda boda rider, from the farmer to the teacher.

With over 25 years of leadership experience across government and the private sector, he understands what it takes to deliver real change. He has served at the highest levels of government, including as Chief of Staff and Head of the Presidential Delivery Unit, where he drove accountability and results across Kenya's public service.

Our Vision for Machakos

A comprehensive 5-pillar plan to transform Machakos into a model African county by 2032

Smart Governance

Implement digital systems for transparency, efficient service delivery, and citizen engagement through technology-driven solutions.

  • Digital County Services Portal
  • E-Procurement & Transparency Systems
  • Citizen Feedback & Participation Platforms
  • Real-time Service Delivery Tracking

Economic Empowerment

Support youth entrepreneurship, SMEs, and create inclusive business policies to stimulate job creation and economic growth.

  • 50,000 Youth Jobs Creation Program
  • SME Development Fund & Support
  • Machakos Innovation & Tech Hub
  • Business Registration Simplified

Infrastructure & Mobility

Upgrade roads, public transport, and housing solutions to address Machakos' growing urban mobility challenges.

  • Modernized Public Transport System
  • Road Network Upgrade & Maintenance
  • Affordable Housing Program
  • Pedestrian & Cycling Infrastructure

Safety & Security

Neighborhood policing, modern surveillance systems, and community safety programs to make Machakos safer for all residents.

  • Neighborhood Policing Program
  • Modern Surveillance Systems
  • Community Safety Programs
  • Street Lighting Initiative
  • Youth Engagement Programs

Environment & Sustainability

Green spaces expansion, waste management reforms, and climate-conscious urban planning for a cleaner, greener Machakos.

  • Green Spaces Expansion
  • Waste Management Reforms
  • Climate-Conscious Urban Planning
  • Tree Planting Initiative
  • Water Conservation Programs
